Privacy Policy

O'Brien Field Docs for iOS

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

Overview

O'Brien Field Docs ("the App") is an internal enterprise tool developed by O'Brien Systems for field documentation, blueprint annotation, and project management. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

The App collects the following information to provide its core functionality:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address, obtained through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) authentication with your @obriensys.com organizational account.
  • Project Data: Projects, blueprints, pins, observations, notes, checklists, quotes, and other content you create within the App. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Photos and Media: Photos and videos you capture or import to document field observations. These are stored locally on your device and are only uploaded when you explicitly use the backup or sync features.
  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant permission, the App may record location coordinates associated with observations for reference purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: Your Microsoft Entra ID credentials are used solely to verify your identity and authorize access to the App.
  • App Functionality: Project data, photos, and observations are used to provide the core features of the App including blueprint annotation, reporting, and quote generation.
  • AI Features: When you use the AI design assistant, your project context may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. No personal information beyond project content is included in these requests.
  • Cloud Backup: If you enable OneDrive backup, your project data is synced to your organization's Microsoft OneDrive account.

Data Storage and Security

All project data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage provided by iOS. Cloud backups are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in your organization's Microsoft 365 tenant, subject to your organization's security policies.

Third-Party Services

  • Microsoft Entra ID: Used for authentication. Subject to Microsoft's Privacy Statement.
  • Anthropic Claude API: Used for AI design assistance features. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft OneDrive: Used for optional cloud backup. Subject to your organization's Microsoft 365 policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as required for the App's functionality.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Cloud backups are retained according to your organization's data retention policies.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You can also delete your local data by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
