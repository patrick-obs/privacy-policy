OBrien Field Docs

Privacy Policy

Internal Use Application

OBrien Field Docs (“the App”) is published by O’Brien Systems (“we”, “us”). The App is a field-documentation tool for our staff and authorized users to document site walks, blueprints, punch lists, room scans, quotes, and related project work. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it goes.

Who can use the App

Access requires signing in with a Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) account on the @obriensys.com domain. The App is intended for O’Brien Systems personnel and authorized partners, not the general public.

Information we handle

Account & identity (via Microsoft Entra ID)

When you sign in, we receive your name, work email, and job title from Microsoft to identify you and control access. Authentication tokens are stored securely in the device Keychain. We never see or store your Microsoft password.

Project & field data you create

Projects, blueprints, pins, observations, checklists, quotes, expenses, contacts, calendar items, and notes. This is stored locally on your device (SwiftData) and, if enabled, backed up to your organization’s Microsoft OneDrive.

Photos, videos & voice notes

Photos and videos you capture or import for documentation, and voice notes you record. Voice notes may be transcribed using on-device/Apple speech recognition.

Room scans (LiDAR)

On supported devices, the App uses the LiDAR sensor to scan rooms and generate floor plans and measurements. Scan data is stored with the associated project.

Microsoft 365 data (only with your consent)

With your permission, the App can read your Outlook contacts and recent email metadata via Microsoft Graph to help you import business contacts and identify potential leads into the CRM. This requires explicit Microsoft consent to the relevant permissions and is used only to build the in-app proposals you review and approve.

How we use third-party services

Anthropic (Claude AI)

AI features — photo/voice classification, design assistance, receipt scanning, and text improvement — send the relevant content (text and/or images) to Anthropic’s API to generate results. Free-text we send is filtered to remove obvious personal identifiers (emails, phone numbers, SSNs) before transmission. Images may contain whatever is in frame. Anthropic processes this data to return a result and does not use API data to train its models. See Anthropic’s privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Microsoft (Entra ID, Graph, OneDrive)

Used for sign-in, optional contact import, and optional cloud backup, as described above. Governed by Microsoft’s privacy terms.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Device permissions the App requests

  • Camera — photograph punch-list items and field conditions.
  • Microphone — record voice notes for observations.
  • Speech recognition — transcribe voice notes.
  • Photo library — import inspection photos and save annotated photos.
  • LiDAR / world sensing — scan rooms to generate floor plans.
  • Face ID — optional app lock to protect project data.

Each permission is requested only when first needed, and the App functions (with that feature unavailable) if you decline.

Data storage, retention & security

Your data lives primarily on your device and, if backup is enabled, in your organization’s OneDrive. Authentication tokens are kept in the device Keychain. Network traffic to Anthropic and Microsoft is encrypted in transit (TLS). Retention follows O’Brien Systems’ internal data-retention practices; data is removed when a project is deleted or when you sign out and clear local data, subject to backup copies retained per company policy.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time to end the session on the device.
  • Decline any device permission (camera, mic, photos, LiDAR, Face ID).
  • Decline Microsoft Graph contact-import consent.
  • Avoid AI features if you prefer not to send content to Anthropic; the App provides manual alternatives for AI-assisted actions.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us (below).

Children

The App is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Material changes will be communicated to users.
