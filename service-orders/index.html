OBS Service Orders

Privacy Policy

Internal Use Application

OBS Service Orders (“the App”) is published by O’Brien Systems (“we”, “us”). The App is a field-service tool for our staff and authorized users to create and manage service orders, preventive-maintenance visits, customers, equipment, and related work. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it goes.

Who can use the App

Access requires signing in with a Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) account on the @obriensys.com domain, or with a local staff account issued by an O’Brien Systems administrator. The App is intended for O’Brien Systems personnel and authorized partners, not the general public.

Information we handle

Account & identity

When you sign in with Microsoft, we receive your name, work email, and directory identifiers from Microsoft to identify you and control access. Authentication tokens are stored securely in the device Keychain; we never see or store your Microsoft password. Local staff accounts store a username and a salted, hashed password (never plaintext) on the device.

Service & customer data you create

Service orders, preventive-maintenance events, customer records (names, site addresses, contacts, phone/email), equipment and site details, parts/inventory, labor and pricing, and notes. This is stored locally on your device and, if SharePoint sync is enabled by your organization, backed up to your organization’s Microsoft 365 document library.

Photos & signatures

Photos you capture or attach to a service order for documentation, and handwritten technician and customer signatures you capture on-device to sign off on completed work. These are stored with the associated service order.

Location / addresses

To place customer sites on a map and suggest nearby scheduled work, the App converts customer street addresses to map coordinates using Apple’s geocoding service. The App does not continuously track your device’s location.

How we use third-party services

Microsoft (Entra ID, Graph, SharePoint / OneDrive)

Used for sign-in and, if enabled by your organization, to sync service orders to your company’s Microsoft 365 document library. Governed by Microsoft’s privacy terms.

Apple (maps & geocoding)

Customer addresses are sent to Apple’s geocoding/maps service to obtain coordinates for the map and nearby-work features. Governed by Apple’s privacy terms.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Device permissions the App requests

  • Camera — photograph equipment and field conditions to attach to service orders.
  • Photo library — import photos to attach to service orders.

Each permission is requested only when first needed, and the App functions (with that feature unavailable) if you decline.

Data storage, retention & security

Your data lives primarily on your device and, if sync is enabled, in your organization’s Microsoft 365 document library. Authentication tokens are kept in the device Keychain; identity and credential files are stored with file-level data protection. Network traffic to Microsoft and Apple is encrypted in transit (TLS). Retention follows O’Brien Systems’ internal data-retention practices; data is removed when a record is deleted or when you sign out and clear local data, subject to backup copies retained per company policy.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time to end the session on the device.
  • Decline any device permission (camera, photos).
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us (below).

Children

The App is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Material changes will be communicated to users.
